### T003. "Why does Template12 fetch the product and scheme three different ways? Is that not a bug?"

**Strong answer:** It is a known and documented trade-off, "CRITICAL FACT #1" in the S12 javadoc (about lines 77-87). The three fetches copy three different Oracle behaviours:
- `fetchTemplate12PolicyDocumentMain` (PolicyDocument): throws `IllegalStateException` if WIP and live data disagree. It copies Oracle error ORA-01427.
- `PlineT12PolicyDocumentHandler` (PolicyDocument2): prefers posted data, falls back to WIP with `$ifNull`, no conflict detection, calls REST for `EINV_PRINT_IND` and `SHOW_STAX_INV_IND`.
- `Template12LandscapeDispatchHandler`: product from WIP, then SB, then `UWGE_POLICY_CONTRACTS` (a third fallback that Jasper does not have).
They were not unified, because that would change PolicyDocument2 behaviour without product sign-off. Cost: the same contract can behave differently in each part.

**Weak answer:** "It was copy-paste." Or "I would merge them all now."

**Red flags:** Says "it is the same query". Cannot name what ORA-01427 is.

**Follow-up questions:**
- What is ORA-01427? (Oracle: single-row subquery returns more than one row.)
- What would you do to unify them? (One shared product-context object, with business sign-off. Inferred.)
- Which one fails the whole request? (PolicyDocument primary; it has no try/catch.)

### T006. "What is `mergeSingleTransDet` and why would a reviewer worry about it?"

**Strong answer:** In B12 (about line 189). It copies every non-null field of a new single-row band onto every existing `transDet`, using reflection. It exists so that single-row bands do not multiply rows. Multi-row bands use `addSingleTransDet`. Worries: (1) last non-null writer wins, so a later band can overwrite a scalar set by an earlier band; (2) band boundaries are lost inside one envelope; (3) reflection is slow and hides bugs; (4) `endorsementDetails` is "last call wins". The history shows fixes: `7399d2fc1` "mergenonnullvalues" (OPUSM-42045) and `c0c6d449a` "dynamic merging".

**Weak answer:** "It merges lists." Or "It is just a helper."

**Red flags:** No mention of reflection or overwrite risk.

**Follow-up questions:**
- Better design? (Explicit mapping per band, or one reportData per band. Landscape rows already use one `reportData` each via `toLandscapeReportData`.)
- Is there a unit test? (Yes, the `mergeSingleTransDet_*` tests in the base service test.)
